Analytics enablement workflow

1. Frame problemClarify business question, commercial objective, constraints and success measure.
2. Integrate dataCombine source systems, customer attributes, communications and response signals.
3. ValidateClean, reconcile and document assumptions, definitions, exclusions and quality controls.
4. AnalyseApply segmentation, trend analysis, scenario tests and statistical reasoning.
5. RecommendTranslate evidence into a clear action, risk, expected impact and next test.
